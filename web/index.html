About and safety

This tool decodes nameplates from a local knowledge base and reasons over the readings you enter. It does not replace the manufacturer's IOM, wiring diagram or service bulletin — confirm anything marked "verify on nameplate".

Never bypass safeties (HPS, limits, rollout, gas pressure switches, A2L mitigation). Charge only with valid readings after a leak check. LOTO per NFPA 70E; CAT III/IV meters on 480/575 V; A2L refrigerants need rated recovery gear and no ignition sources.

Photos you attach are sent to the assistant for analysis and stored with the conversation; no analytics or tracking.
